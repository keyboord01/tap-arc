Three steps, then the contract does the rest.

1

Fill your vault

Deposit USDC from your wallet. Anything that isn't spent stays yours to withdraw, whenever you want.

2

Open a tap

Choose an address and a rule: how much, how often it resets, and an end date if you want one.

3

Let them spend

They can spend up to the limit and not a cent more. Pause, edit, or revoke the tap in one transaction.

One vault, a tap for everyone.

Each spender gets their own rule, and nobody can touch anyone else's.

Why it runs on Arc

An allowance only works if spending it is instant and cheap. Arc makes both the default.

USDC pays the gas

Spenders only ever hold USDC. Nobody has to buy a second token before they can use their allowance.

Final the moment it lands

Arc finalizes a transaction as soon as it's included in a block. No reorgs and no confirmation countdown.

Fees small enough for small budgets

A spend costs about a tenth of a cent, so a 5 USDC daily allowance still makes sense.
